Project Overview

Scaling revenue requires reps to manage more clients, often leading to lost oversight, missed opportunities, and unnoticed at-risk accounts. Oz is an AI agent that eliminates this by automating administrative busywork and algorithmically prioritizing daily tasks. This empowers reps to step away from the CRM and focus purely on building relationships.

The Challenge & Solution

The Goal

Drive revenue growth by significantly expanding the client portfolio each rep can effectively manage.

The Bottleneck

Human cognitive limits. Reps can't manually track an ever-growing portfolio, resulting in missed opportunities and at-risk accounts slipping through the cracks.

The Solution

An agentic co-pilot that solves the scale problem in 3 core steps:

Smart Prioritization

It's impossible to manually track the exact status of hundreds of clients at any given moment. The system analyzes account signals to actively surface immediate needs, guiding the rep's attention to the most urgent opportunities.

Critical
Critical
Critical

Automating Admin

Eradicating the 30-40% time-drain by autonomously drafting follow-up emails, quotes, and updating Salesforce.

Transforming tedious data entry into a simple review and approve workflow.

Relationship-Driven Revenue

With busywork eliminated, reps redirect their energy into nurturing clients. This human touch is exactly what enables them to successfully handle a larger client base and ultimately drive revenue.

02

Chat on the right

We intentionally placed the chat on the right.

User mental models dictate that left-aligned panels act as “Builders” (structural changes), while right-aligned panels act as “Assistants” (support and contextual actions).

03

AI Drafts, Humans Approve

Users aren't ready to let AI independently contact clients.

The V1 strategy revolves around “Drafting” — the agent does the heavy lifting, but the human must ratify every action. Full automation is our North Star, to be unlocked only after trust is solidified.

Main screens

Catch Up

Acting as a daily command center, this screen surfaces a scannable feed of prioritized signals and AI-drafted tasks. It enables a frictionless "review and approve" flow, instantly freeing reps to focus on actual selling.

Send Email

The agent leverages deep relationship context and real-time sentiment to draft the exact right message at the perfect time. Reps are simply required to review and approve, ensuring highly personalized outreach with zero manual writing.

Portfolio

Rep’s portfolio moves beyond static lists by surfacing real-time relationship health and actionable insights across hundreds of accounts. It empowers reps to manage a massive portfolio confidently, providing instant visibility into where their strategic focus is needed most.

Create opportunity

By analyzing client conversations, the agent autonomously generates and populates new CRM opportunities. This eliminates a major Sisyphean pain point, transforming tedious post-call data entry into a zero-effort, automated workflow.

Update opportunity

Instead of manually logging deal progress after every touchpoint, the agent auto-drafts opportunity updates based on the latest conversational insights. This transforms ongoing CRM maintenance into a frictionless "review and approve" workflow.
